How a campaign runs

From setup to follow-up in 5 steps

  1. 1

    Set up

    We create or fix your WhatsApp Business profile, catalogue and auto-replies.

  2. 2

    Collect contacts

    We add chat links and QR codes so happy customers can join your list with permission.

  3. 3

    Plan messages

    We prepare a simple monthly plan of offers, reminders and festive wishes.

  4. 4

    Send & reply

    Messages go out on schedule, and enquiries get answered quickly.

  5. 5

    Review

    Every month we review replies and bookings and improve the next plan.

✅ Done the right way

  • We message only people who agreed to hear from you
  • Every list has an easy way to opt out
  • No spam, no bulk messages to strangers
  • We follow WhatsApp Business rules

📊 What we track

  • Messages sent and replies received
  • Enquiries turned into bookings
  • Repeat customers
  • Shared with you in a simple monthly report
